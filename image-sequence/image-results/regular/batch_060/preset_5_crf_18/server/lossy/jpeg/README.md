# JPEG (quality 100): regular_batch_060, preset 5 CRF 18

Generated by `scripts/image_formats.py` from the server's report (`server --extract-images`, [`images.json`](../../images.json)); do not edit. One of the [lossy formats](../README.md), in [the comparison of the image formats](../../README.md).

## The format

For comparison: a standard JFIF file any viewer shows in the right colors, as close to the frame as JPEG allows. JPEG's YCbCr is BT.601 in full range, so each frame is converted to it from BT.709 in limited range, then written at quality 100 (every quantization step 1, the least JPEG can lose) in 4:2:0, its U and V planes passed as they are, with no RGB or resampling in the encoder (`jpeg-encoder` crate). It is read back with FFmpeg's decoder, which also gives the 4:2:0 planes as they are, then converted back. What is lost: the rounding of the two conversions and of the DCT.

Settings: JFIF, BT.601 full range, 4:2:0, quality 100; FFmpeg's decoder. The files are `output/.../images/jpeg/frame_<k>.*`, one per frame of the video, 60 here, and each one was read back and compared with its frame: see [what it loses](#what-it-loses).

## All the images, against the AV1 video

The time for all 60 images, against the same step on the AV1 video, the reference: the gray bar, first.

**Encoding.** Writing all 60 images takes 16.76 s, 1.8x slower than the 9.45 s SVT-AV1 worked to encode the video on the server (its working time, without its waits for decoded PNGs). The two do not do the same work: SVT-AV1 compresses heavily and loses detail (preset 5 CRF 18), while every image here is a frame already decoded, converted. The comparison says what each step costs, not which is more efficient.

![JPEG (quality 100): encoding all the images, against the AV1 video](encode-time.svg)

**Decoding.** Reading all 60 images back takes 7.40 s, 6.3x slower than the 1.17 s dav1d takes to decode the whole AV1 video. The images are read one by one, each a file of its own; the video in one pass, its frames depending on each other, so a client wanting any single frame of the video must decode from the keyframe before it.

![JPEG (quality 100): decoding all the images, against the AV1 video](decode-time.svg)

## Several images at a time

Every format is also written and read back 4 images at a time (one image leaves most cores idle): all 60 images, then all of them read back. One image takes as long as before; what changes is the whole sequence: it takes less.

|---|---|---|---|
| Encoding | 16.76 s | 8.14 s | 2.1x faster |
| Decoding | 7.40 s | 4.61 s | 1.6x faster |
